# Privacy Policy — TranscriptNotes **Last updated: September 2026** TranscriptNotes ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. ## Data We Collect **None.** The Extension does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. ## How It Works - The Extension runs entirely inside your browser - Transcripts are fetched directly from YouTube's public caption API (the same one YouTube uses on its own site) - All processing happens locally on your device - No data is sent to any external server ## Permissions The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions: | Permission | Reason | |-----------|--------| | `activeTab` | To detect when you are on a YouTube video page | | `scripting` | To extract the video transcript from the YouTube page | | `storage` | To remember your daily credit count (stored locally only) | ## Contact Questions?
