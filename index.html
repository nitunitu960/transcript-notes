FAQ

Common questions

Does it work on all YouTube videos?
It works on any YouTube video that has captions or auto-generated subtitles enabled. Most videos do — you'll see a CC icon in the YouTube player.
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything runs inside your browser. No text or video data is sent to any server. The extension only requests permission to run on youtube.com pages.
What does the free plan include?
1 summary generation per day (resets at midnight). Transcript extraction is always unlimited and free.
How do I upgrade to Pro?
When your daily limit is reached, click "Upgrade to Pro" in the extension popup. You'll be taken to our secure checkout powered by Dodo Payments.
